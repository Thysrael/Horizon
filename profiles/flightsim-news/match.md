# Flight simulation news profile

Use this profile for timely developments in consumer flight simulation: Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 and 2024, X-Plane, Prepar3D, DCS World, and the add-on, hardware, and community ecosystem around them.

Typical items include simulator updates and patches (Sim Updates, World Updates, betas), aircraft and airport add-on announcements, releases and major updates, freeware releases, utilities and tools, flight-sim hardware (yokes, throttles, pedals, panels, VR and head tracking), online networks and virtual airlines (VATSIM, IVAO), marketplace and platform changes (PC, Xbox, PlayStation), developer roadmaps, and community events such as FSExpo or FSWeekend.

Do not use this profile for real-world commercial or military aviation news without a simulation angle, general video-game news, screenshots or videos shared without new information, individual support questions, liveries or minor repaints, or promotional posts and giveaways without substantive information.
