# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how much a flight-simulation news item matters to hobbyist simmers who fly regularly on PC or console and follow the add-on ecosystem.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Must know.** A new simulator or major simulator update shipping, a long-awaited flagship aircraft releasing, a platform-wide change (pricing, marketplace, console availability, licensing), or an incident that breaks or blocks the simulator for many users.
- **7-8: High value.** Release or major update of a study-level or widely used aircraft, a major airport or scenery release, a substantial freeware release, a significant utility or hardware launch, a confirmed release date or detailed roadmap from a major developer, or a beta that changes how people fly.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Announcements and previews of upcoming add-ons with concrete details, incremental updates to popular products, regional airports from established developers, notable sales, or event recaps.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Small fixes, minor sceneries, teasers without details, repaints, routine development-update posts with nothing new, or reposts of information already widely covered.
- **0-2: Noise.** Screenshots or videos without news, support questions, giveaways, off-topic real-world aviation, or pure promotion.

# Evaluation guidance

Weigh how many simmers are affected, whether something is actually available now versus merely announced, the reputation and track record of the developer, compatibility across MSFS 2020, MSFS 2024, and consoles, price and freeware status, and the concreteness of the details. A shipped release outranks a teaser of the same product. Treat substantive community reports, such as performance, bugs, or realism feedback, as additional evidence of value, but do not equate upvotes with importance. Do not reward hype wording.

Use three to five specific topic tags, including the simulator platform and the add-on type or developer when known.
