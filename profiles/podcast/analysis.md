interviews with several grounded insights; 5–6 for mixed or mostly familiar
material; 0–4 for promotion, repetition, speculation without reasoning, or very
little substance. Guest authority can inform expertise but cannot replace the
actual content. Distinguish a speaker's opinion or prediction from established
fact. Judge the whole supplied evidence, including the closing sections.
