Use this profile for a podcast episode, interview, or substantial spoken
conversation with a supplied transcript. Its purpose is to convey the speakers'
reasoning, experiences, disagreements, and concrete examples in a reading brief.
The topic may be technology, science, business, culture, or another domain.

Do not match an article merely mentioning a podcast, an episode announcement,
show notes, a promotional excerpt, or a short clip. Those do not provide enough
evidence for an episode-level brief. A technical tutorial written as an article
belongs to the technology-blog profile even when its author also has a podcast.
