# Startups & innovation profile

Use this profile for news about startups, venture capital and the innovation ecosystem: funding rounds, acquisitions and exits, new products from young companies, founder and growth playbooks, accelerator and incubator programmes, public innovation policy (Bpifrance, France 2030, EU programmes), and AI products being adopted by startups and SMEs.

Prefer French and European ecosystem news, but include major US or global startup news when it changes the landscape.

Do not use this profile for pure stock-market coverage, large-corporate earnings, deep technical research without a business angle, or promotional press releases with no concrete facts.
