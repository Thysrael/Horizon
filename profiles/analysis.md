# Evaluation goal

Evaluate the importance of timely news for a reader who runs startup acceleration programmes, coaches founders, teaches entrepreneurship and follows AI product trends, with a focus on France and Europe.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Landscape-changing.** Very large rounds or exits, new funding schemes or regulations that affect many startups, a new category-defining product, or a major shift in VC behaviour.
- **7-8: High value.** Notable French/European rounds (roughly Series A and above), meaningful acquisitions, new accelerator or public programmes, concrete founder playbooks with data, fast-growing AI products relevant to SMEs.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Seed rounds in promising sectors, useful case studies, moderate product launches, ecosystem events worth knowing.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Routine announcements, generic advice, listicles, rankings without method.
- **0-2: Noise.** Pure promotion, spam, off-topic content.

# Evaluation guidance

Reward concrete facts (amounts, investors, metrics, dates, names) and relevance to founders, investors or programme managers. Give a small bonus to news about the Hauts-de-France / Lille ecosystem. Do not reward hype or vague "AI will change everything" claims.

Use three to five specific topic tags.
